# Standards, ESG and fabrics owner update

Updated on 5 October 2026 in both TH and EN.

## Applied scope

- Four standards/document highlights: Big C Grade A (94.61%, Pass, 2025), Lotus's Blue Tier, Category 2 factory notification and local operating licence.
- Audit highlights use text only, with no audit logos or PDF links. Annual audit wording is retained for Big C.

Previously supplied staff welfare details are retained. No transport, scrap recycling or emissions figures were added.
- All eight fabric cards now include the supplied applications, properties and specific Organic/Recycled alternatives. Rayon includes Songkran and Hawaiian shirts, LENZING™ ECOVERO™ and FSC-certified Viscose.
- Fabric certification relates to sourced fabrics; availability remains subject to Specification, MOQ and sourcing.
