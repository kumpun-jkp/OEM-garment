EN

Quality checked before it reaches you

Fabric inspection before cutting, colour checks in our colour room and needle detection for every garment before packing.

Before production · Fabric and colour checks

Use the fabric inspection machine to check for defects before cutting, and review fabric colour in the colour room against the agreed requirements.

During production · In-line QC

Sample garments during sewing to check measurements, seams, stitches and details against the approved sample.

After production · End-line QC

Inspect every completed garment, correct and recheck as needed, then pass every garment through the needle detector before packing.

Can I get a sample first?
